title: Limitations and roadmap

This is the walking skeleton: the bets are proven (virtualization on both axes, scroll scaling,
windows, the keyboard, any structure), and much of a full grid is still to come.

## The gaps

| missing | what the examples do meanwhile |
| --- | --- |
| The header needs a **background and a `z-index`** to cover the rows that scroll under it, and so do the summary rows. | `z-10` and an opaque background on `Header` and `Summary`. |
| The **summary rows' figures** are yours: the grid computes no total, count or average. | The figures computed over the rows the app shows, read in `renderSummaryCell` ([Summary rows](/docs/concepts/summary-rows)). |
| **Selecting every row** of a server's data: with `getRow`, a row not loaded has no key, and "select every row" refuses. | A middleware answers `selected-rows.select-all` with the server's own ([Row selection](/docs/concepts/row-selection)). |
| **One range of cells** at a time: Ctrl+click adds no second one, and a click on a header selects no column. A copy reads **every cell of the range**, so a range of millions of cells makes a text that large. One value pasted over a larger range lands in its first cell. | Your own fill in `onRangePaste`, reading the range ([Cell selection](/docs/concepts/cell-selection)). |
| Sorting, filtering and pages **in memory need every row**: `useLocalRows` takes an array, not `getRow`. | Past what the browser should hold, a server does it ([`server-side`](/examples/server-side)). |
| Every **nested grid** is a tab stop by default. | The inner grid's tab stops only while the outer cell or row holding it is active ([Nested grids](/docs/guides/nested-grids), [`master-detail`](/examples/master-detail)). |
| **Sorted rows do not move**: a row dropped in a sorted grid would not stay where it was dropped, so the grid refuses the move. | A status that says so and a button that clears the sort ([Row reordering](/docs/concepts/row-reordering), [`row-reordering`](/examples/row-reordering)). |
| A **tree in memory** is not grouped too (`groupBy` is not read with `getSubRows`), and its filters do not open the parents of what they find. | `expandAll()` after a search ([Tree data](/docs/concepts/tree-data)). |
| **Row groups** are your data's: the grid shows the rows it is given and groups nothing itself. Grouped rows do not move by drag, and a page of grouped rows in memory is of the rows shown, a group's rows possibly on the next page. | `useLocalRows`'s `groupBy` in memory, or a server's flattened rows ([Row grouping](/docs/concepts/row-grouping)). |
| **Measured heights** make the scrollbar approximate until the rows are measured: a jump to a far position lands near it. | An `estimatedRowHeight` near the typical row ([Measured heights](/docs/concepts/measured-heights)). |
| A wheel over a nested grid that has reached its end chains to the outer grid natively: under the outer grid's **scroll scaling**, that step is a scaled jump. | Nothing: nested grids in the examples are not scaled. |
| `DataGrid.Empty` has **no structure of its own**: inside a grid, its content needs a row and a cell. | `role="row"` and `role="gridcell"` wrappers, or `tr` and `td` in a table ([Empty state](/docs/guides/empty-state)). |
| A **fit to content** measures the rendered rows only: rows scrolled into view later may be wider. A cell spanning columns is never measured. | Your own width for every row, set with `column-widths.set` ([Automatic widths](/docs/concepts/auto-widths)). |
| A group's handle cannot move a **flex column that does not resize**: the group ends off the pointer. | Make such a column `resizable`, or keep it out of a resizable group ([Automatic widths](/docs/concepts/auto-widths#a-group-with-a-column-that-flexes-and-does-not-resize)). |

## The roadmap

The next steps, each its own release, in this order:

<Steps>

<Step>

## Row operations

Done: [sorting](/docs/concepts/sorting), single and multi, as grid state, and
[rows in memory](/docs/concepts/rows-in-memory): sorted, filtered, searched and paged by one
opt-in hook, which a server-driven grid never loads.

</Step>

<Step>

## Columns

Done: [column groups](/docs/concepts/column-groups) with multi-row headers,
[pinned columns](/docs/concepts/pinned-columns) at the start and at the end, a
[right-to-left](/docs/concepts/right-to-left) direction mirroring every column feature,
[column resizing](/docs/concepts/column-resizing) by drag and keyboard, within minimum and maximum
widths, [column reordering](/docs/concepts/column-reordering) of columns and whole groups by
drag and keyboard, [automatic widths](/docs/concepts/auto-widths): flex columns and columns
fitted to their content, [column spanning](/docs/concepts/column-spanning) in the body and
the header, and [collapsible groups](/docs/concepts/collapsible-groups) with labels that stay in
view.

</Step>

<Step>

## Selection and editing

Started: [row selection](/docs/concepts/row-selection), one row or many, by your checkboxes and the
grid's keys, and [cell selection](/docs/concepts/cell-selection), a range of cells by the keys or
a drag, copied and pasted as tab-separated text, and [editing](/docs/concepts/editing) with your
own editors, and a [fill handle](/docs/concepts/fill) whose values are yours.

</Step>

<Step>

## Rows beyond a flat list

Started: [master-detail](/docs/guides/master-detail), rows that expand into an area holding
another grid or any component, [summary rows](/docs/concepts/summary-rows) under the header
and at the bottom edge, rows and details as tall as their content
([measured heights](/docs/concepts/measured-heights)), and
[row reordering](/docs/concepts/row-reordering) by a handle and the keys, and
[row grouping](/docs/concepts/row-grouping) by columns with aggregates, and
[tree data](/docs/concepts/tree-data), in memory or listed by a server as it opens.

</Step>

</Steps>
