This is the walking skeleton: the bets are proven (virtualization on both axes, scroll scaling,
windows, the keyboard, any structure), and much of a full grid is still to come.

## The gaps

| missing | what the examples do meanwhile |
| --- | --- |
| **Pinned columns**: a row-number column scrolls away with the rest. | Nothing: the [`large-dataset`](/examples/large-dataset) row column scrolls. |
| The header needs a **background and a `z-index`** to cover the rows that scroll under it. | `z-10` and an opaque background on `Header`. |
| No built-in **selection or filtering**: the grid shows the rows it is given. | The app keeps that state and passes the rows: [`companies`](/examples/companies) selects, [`tasks`](/examples/tasks) filters by facets. |
| **Sorting** is state only: the grid keeps the sort, never orders the rows (no local pipeline yet). | The app orders the rows it passes by `sortColumns` ([`sorting`](/examples/sorting), [`companies`](/examples/companies)). |
| **Controls inside cells** (a checkbox, a link, a button) are tab stops of their own: Tab walks through them before it leaves the grid. | A `tabIndex` of -1 unless their cell is active, read from `useGridView().active` ([`companies`](/examples/companies)). |
| Every **nested grid** is a tab stop by default, and its rows have the outer row's fixed height (no master and detail yet). | The inner grid's tab stops only while the outer cell holding it is active ([Nested grids](/docs/guides/nested-grids)); a row height that fits the inner grid. |
| A wheel over a nested grid that has reached its end chains to the outer grid natively: under the outer grid's **scroll scaling**, that step is a scaled jump. | Nothing: nested grids in the examples are not scaled. |
| `DataGrid.Empty` has **no structure of its own**: inside a grid, its content needs a row and a cell. | `role="row"` and `role="gridcell"` wrappers, or `tr` and `td` in a table ([Empty state](/docs/guides/empty-state)). |
| A **group's label** scrolls with its group: in a group wider than the view, it can be out of sight (no sticky labels yet). Under scaled column scroll, a group's cell is cut to the rendered columns, so its label moves with the column window. | A label centred on the group, groups no wider than a few views ([`grouped-headers`](/examples/grouped-headers)). |
| Groups cannot be **collapsed**, and the body has no **column spans**. | Nothing yet: both are later steps of the Columns roadmap. |

## The roadmap

The next steps, each its own release, in this order:

<Steps>

<Step>

## Row operations

Started: [sorting](/docs/concepts/sorting), single and multi, as grid state your app orders its
rows by. Next: an opt-in local pipeline for data in memory (which a server-driven grid never pays
for), filtering and pagination.

</Step>

<Step>

## Columns

Started: [column groups](/docs/concepts/column-groups) with multi-row headers. Next: pinned
columns (start and end), column spans in the body, sticky group labels, collapsing groups,
reordering by drag and keyboard, resizing with minimum and maximum widths, automatic widths.

</Step>

<Step>

## Selection and editing

Row and cell-range selection, a selection column, editing, copy and paste, fill, summary rows.

</Step>

<Step>

## Rows beyond a flat list

Row grouping, tree data, master and detail, heights measured from the content, row reordering.

</Step>

</Steps>
